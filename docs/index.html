Common questions

Where are my screenshots stored?
Imported screenshots and their recognized text stay in ScreenStash's local app storage.

Does deleting an item remove it from Photos?
No. Deleting an item from ScreenStash does not delete the original image from your Photos library.

How do I remove all ScreenStash data?
Open ScreenStash, choose Settings, and select Delete All App Data.
